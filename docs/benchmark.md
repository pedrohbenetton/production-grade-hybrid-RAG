## Notes

- Metrics are computed per document: a document's rank is its best chunk's rank. P@k divides by k, so with ~1 relevant document per query the ceiling of P@10 is ≈0.1; P@1, R@10, MRR@10 and nDCG@10 are the discriminating columns.
- Latency is the retrieval call only (query embedding, search, fusion, rerank), measured sequentially on CPU after an untimed warm-up; it is hardware-dependent.
- Reranking sees the fused top-30 chunks and returns the top 20 chunks, collapsed to the top 10 documents.
